South Korea’s 2026 E-Commerce Act Amendments — Private-Seller Information, Review Policies and Platform Liability

The amendments to South Korea’s Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. (the “E-Commerce Act”), effective on 21 July 2026, revise information-verification and dispute-cooperation duties for private-party transaction intermediaries, consumer-review operating disclosures, consent decisions and temporary suspension orders.
