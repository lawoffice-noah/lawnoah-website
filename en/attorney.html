PROFILE

Commercial, regulatory,
and dispute experience.

Bo-Gyeong Kim is the Managing Attorney of Law Office Noah. She previously served with the International Dispute Settlement Division of the Korean Ministry of Justice, where she supported international arbitration matters, legal research and strategy work, and analysis of arbitral awards and evidentiary materials, including ISDS matters.

She later worked as in-house counsel at Interojo Inc., handling domestic and international commercial agreements, corporate and transactional advice, regulatory compliance, M&A-related review, and domestic and international disputes.

At Law Office Noah, she represents individual and business clients directly. Her approach is to review the facts, available evidence, client objectives, procedural options, and practical enforceability together rather than treating a legal issue in isolation.
